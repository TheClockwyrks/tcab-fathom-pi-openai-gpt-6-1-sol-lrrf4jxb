# Fathom

Your light is a lifeline. And a lure. Slip into a flooded maze as a tiny glowing forager, grazing the plankton that hangs in the corridors. Eat every mote to clear a depth and descend into a trench with more hunters and a shorter reach of sonar.

In the Kindle dive, the abyss exists only inside the circle you carry. Your own light reveals what is near; a sonar pulse runs around corners and leaves a memory of the passages ahead. Eating widens your window into the dark. Stop feeding, and that window quietly shrinks again—even familiar ground vanishes beyond its edge.

Three hunters listen for three different mistakes. The Lanternjaw follows your glow, its amber bulb almost indistinguishable from a harmless drifting jellyfish. The eyeless Gloamfin hears your sonar and sends violet echoes through the corridors. The Flarefish lights whole chambers in a sudden bloom, turning a glimpse of safety into a flash of pursuit.

Keep moving, take corners, and decide when knowing the way is worth giving yourself away. Ink can break a sight hunter’s gaze, but sound travels through it. A lost life leaves your discoveries and eaten plankton behind; a cleared maze carries your score deeper. There is always another corridor. There is not always time to see what waits in it.
